# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- Put two fingers on the board and it rolls forward by itself, easing up to a steady speed. You no longer have to push to get going.
- Pushing still works if you want to go faster: keep one finger on the board and swipe the other down the ground.
- The first-time lesson now starts with "Put your pointer and middle fingers on the board to roll".

## 0.1.0
- First version. The board lies on your phone as if the screen were the pavement, and the street rolls and turns underneath it.
- Push: keep your pointer finger on the board and swipe your middle finger down the ground beside it. Hold the ground finger still to brake.
- Steer by sliding your finger on the board a little left or right.
- Ollie: middle finger on the tail, slide your pointer finger up to the nose, fast. Flick off the right side for a kickflip, the left for a heelflip, and swipe the tail finger sideways for a shove-it. Mix them for varials, 360 flips and laser flips. Get your fingers back on the board before it lands to catch it for extra points.
- Grind rails, launch off kickers, and ollie over cones and twigs. Clipping one with your wheels is a bail.
- Tricks chain into combos while you keep popping within a second of landing. Ninety-second sessions, and your best score is kept.
- A short lesson the first time you play (you can skip it, or see it again from the home card).
